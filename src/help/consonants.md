The 44 consonants, by class. The class of the first consonant decides which row
of the tone rules applies.

Each row gathers consonants that sound the same at the start of a syllable.

| Mid | High  |  Low 1  | Low 2 |
| :-: | :---: | :-----: | :---: |
|  ก  |  ข ฃ  |  ค ฅ ฆ  |   ง   |
|  จ  |   ฉ   |   ช ฌ   |  น ณ  |
| ด ฎ |  ถ ฐ  | ท ธ ฑ ฒ |   ม   |
| ต ฏ |   ผ   |   พ ภ   |  ย ญ  |
|  บ  |   ฝ   |    ฟ    |   ร   |
|  ป  | ส ศ ษ |    ซ    |  ล ฬ  |
|  อ  |   ห   |    ฮ    |   ว   |

- **Low 1** consonants sound like the high consonant of their row: they are its
  "twins" (ค and ข, ส and ซ…). Together, a pair can write all five tones.
- **Low 2** consonants have no high twin. A silent ห written before them makes
  them follow the high class rules (หนา, หมู).
- อ at the start of a syllable is silent: it carries a vowel that starts the
  syllable (อา, อิ).
- ฃ and ฅ are obsolete: no modern word uses them, and the game leaves them out.
